Pexeso for Mydli — Privacy Policy
This app does not collect any personal information. Multiplayer functionality uses Firebase Realtime Database (Google) and Ably (real-time messaging service) solely to relay game moves between players in real time. No data is stored permanently. No data is shared with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes.
The app does not require login or registration. No location, contacts, or device identifiers are accessed.
